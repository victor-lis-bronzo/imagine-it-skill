Daily review playlist

Every day LangClips builds a 5-clip queue from the phrases the user missed the most, and shows how the session went.

Source: fictional feature idea (conversation)Date: 2026-11-08Mode: hi-fi

Spec summary

The app picks the 5 phrases the user got wrong most often and turns them into a short playlist of clips. The user plays the clips in order with visible progress, then sees a result screen with the score and what improved. With fewer than 5 mistakes there is no playlist and an empty state explains why.

Flow

  1. 01

    Daily review queue

    Today's 5 clips, progress and the Start button.

    States: default, empty

  2. 02

    Daily result

    Score and phrase-by-phrase outcome after the playlist.

    States: default

Assumptions

  • The entry point is a banner on My Decks.
  • A phrase leaves the list after 2 correct answers in a row.
  • Streak and "Retry missed phrase" are extras not in the spec.
  • Sample phrases and decks are invented.

Out of scope

  • The clip player itself and the exercise screens.
  • Notifications or reminders.

Open questions

  • How is "missed the most" counted: all time or the last 7 days?
